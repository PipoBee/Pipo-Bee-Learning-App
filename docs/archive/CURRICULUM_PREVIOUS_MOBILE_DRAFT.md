# PhonicsQuest — Master Curriculum Specification (15 Levels)

This document defines the 15 foundational levels of synthetic phonics across 5 worlds. Each level includes target phonemes, high-frequency decodable CVC/blend words, tactile stroke tracing data, and task specifications.

---

## World 1: Island of First Sounds
**Theme:** Tropical Island  
**Focus:** High-utility consonants & short vowel /æ/ (s, a, t, p, i, n)

### Level 1: Sound `/s/` — The Hissing Snake
- **Target Sound:** `/s/` (lowercase `s`)
- **Key Words:** `sun`, `snake`, `star`, `sand`
- **Tracing Guidance:** Curve left, slide down, curve right!
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: Identify `/s/` sound and initial sound pictures (`sun` vs `dog`).
  2. Trace the Letter: Lowercase `s`.
  3. Say the Sound: Articulate `/s/` into the microphone.

### Level 2: Sound `/a/` — The Cheerful Apple
- **Target Sound:** `/æ/` (lowercase `a`)
- **Key Words:** `apple`, `ant`, `astronaut`, `axe`
- **Tracing Guidance:** Circle left all around, pull down straight!
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: Identify `/æ/` sound (`apple` vs `banana`).
  2. Trace the Letter: Lowercase `a`.
  3. Sound Matching: Match `a` with `ant` and `apple`.

### Level 3: Sound `/t/` — The Tall Tower
- **Target Sound:** `/t/` (lowercase `t`)
- **Key Words:** `tiger`, `tree`, `train`, `turtle`
- **Blend Introduction:** `sat` (`/s/` + `/a/` + `/t/`)
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: Identify `/t/` sound (`tiger` vs `lion`).
  2. Trace the Letter: Lowercase `t`.
  3. Blend & Read: Blend `/s/` + `/a/` + `/t/` -> `sat`.
  4. Level 1–3 Milestone Test: Composite scored quiz on `s`, `a`, `t`.

## World 2: Valley of Early Blends
**Theme:** Lush Jungle Valley  
**Focus:** Plosives, vowels, and nasal consonants (p, i, n, m, d)

### Level 4: Sound `/p/` — The Popping Popcorn
- **Target Sound:** `/p/` (lowercase `p`)
- **Key Words:** `pig`, `pan`, `pop`, `pin`, `pat`
- **Tracing Guidance:** Pull down below the line, bounce back up and circle right!
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: `/p/` sound vs `/b/` distractor.
  2. Trace the Letter: Lowercase `p`.
  3. Spell the Word: Tap letters to spell `pat`.

### Level 5: Sound `/i/` — The Inky Iguana
- **Target Sound:** `/ɪ/` (lowercase `i`)
- **Key Words:** `igloo`, `ink`, `iguana`, `in`, `it`
- **Blend Words:** `pit`, `tip`, `sip`
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: Short `/ɪ/` vowel recognition.
  2. Trace the Letter: Lowercase `i` (pull down, dot on top).
  3. Blend & Read: Blend `p-i-t` -> `pit`.

### Level 6: Sound `/n/` — The Noisy Net
- **Target Sound:** `/n/` (lowercase `n`)
- **Key Words:** `nest`, `nut`, `net`, `nap`
- **Blend Words:** `pan`, `pin`, `tin`, `tan`
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: `/n/` sound identification.
  2. Trace the Letter: Lowercase `n` (down, up over the tunnel).
  3. Say the Sound: Articulate `/n/`.
  4. World 2 Test: Blending and spelling `pin`, `pan`, `tin`.

## World 3: Mountain of Makers
**Theme:** Rocky Crystal Caverns  
**Focus:** Bilabials, velars, and short vowels (m, d, g, o)

### Level 7: Sound `/m/` — The Munching Monkey
- **Target Sound:** `/m/` (lowercase `m`)
- **Key Words:** `monkey`, `moon`, `map`, `mat`
- **Blend Words:** `mat`, `man`, `sam`
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: `/m/` vs `/n/` auditory discrimination.
  2. Trace the Letter: Lowercase `m` (down, two humps).
  3. Spell the Word: `mat`.

### Level 8: Sound `/d/` — The Drumming Duck
- **Target Sound:** `/d/` (lowercase `d`)
- **Key Words:** `duck`, `drum`, `dog`, `door`
- **Blend Words:** `dad`, `sad`, `dim`
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: `/d/` vs `/b/` discrimination.
  2. Trace the Letter: Lowercase `d` (round the circle, tall stick up, pull down).
  3. Blend & Read: `sad`, `dad`.

### Level 9: Sound `/g/` & `/o/` — The Giggling Goose & Orange
- **Target Sounds:** `/ɡ/` and `/ɒ/` (lowercase `g`, `o`)
- **Key Words:** `goose`, `gift`, `orange`, `otter`
- **Blend Words:** `tag`, `gap`, `pot`, `top`, `dog`
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: Identify `/g/` and `/o/`.
  2. Trace the Letters: Lowercase `g` and `o`.
  3. Blend & Read: `top`, `dog`.
  4. World 3 Test: Multi-sound CVC builder.

## World 4: Forest of Consonants & Digraphs
**Theme:** Enchanted Emerald Forest  
**Focus:** Velar plosives, vowels, and initial blends (c, k, e, u, r)

### Level 10: Sounds `/k/` (`c` & `k`) — The Clever Cat & Kangaroo
- **Target Sound:** `/k/` (spelled `c` and `k`)
- **Key Words:** `cat`, `cup`, `king`, `kite`, `kid`
- **Blend Words:** `cat`, `cup`, `kit`, `cap`
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: Hard `/k/` sound.
  2. Trace the Letters: Lowercase `c` and `k`.
  3. Say the Sound: Articulate `/k/`.

### Level 11: Sound `/e/` — The Energetic Elephant
- **Target Sound:** `/e/` (lowercase `e`)
- **Key Words:** `egg`, `elephant`, `elbow`, `exit`
- **Blend Words:** `pen`, `ten`, `men`, `red`, `pet`
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: Short `/e/` vs `/a/` vowel discrimination.
  2. Trace the Letter: Lowercase `e`.
  3. Blend & Read: `ten`, `pen`.

### Level 12: Sounds `/u/` & `/r/` — The Umbrella Bird & Racing Rabbit
- **Target Sounds:** `/ʌ/` and `/r/` (lowercase `u`, `r`)
- **Key Words:** `umbrella`, `up`, `rabbit`, `ring`, `sun`
- **Blend Words:** `run`, `sun`, `rug`, `rat`, `rip`
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: Short `/ʌ/` and liquid `/r/`.
  2. Trace the Letters: `u` and `r`.
  3. Spell the Word: `run`, `rug`.
  4. World 4 Test: 8-item CVC blending & speech test.

## World 5: Citadel of Consonant Digraphs
**Theme:** Royal Sky Citadel  
**Focus:** H-digraphs and blended words (sh, ch, th, ck, wh)

### Level 13: Digraph `sh` — The Quiet Shell
- **Target Digraph:** `/ʃ/` (`sh`)
- **Key Words:** `ship`, `shell`, `fish`, `shop`, `dish`
- **Blend Words:** `ship`, `fish`, `shop`
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: Identify `/ʃ/` digraph.
  2. Trace Digraph: `s` + `h`.
  3. Say the Sound: Articulate `/ʃ/`.

### Level 14: Digraph `ch` — The Cheerful Chimpanzee
- **Target Digraph:** `/tʃ/` (`ch`)
- **Key Words:** `chin`, `chat`, `chip`, `chop`, `rich`
- **Blend Words:** `chip`, `chin`, `chop`
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: Identify `/tʃ/` sound.
  2. Trace Digraph: `c` + `h`.
  3. Blend & Read: Read aloud `chip` and `chop`.

### Level 15: Grand Master Phonics Quest — Digraph `th` & Graduation
- **Target Digraph:** `/θ/` and `/ð/` (`th`)
- **Key Words:** `thin`, `moth`, `this`, `that`, `path`
- **Comprehensive Blends:** `sat`, `pin`, `dog`, `cat`, `run`, `ship`, `chip`, `this`
- **Tasks:**
  1. Listen & Pick: Voiced vs unvoiced `th`.
  2. Trace Digraph: `t` + `h`.
  3. Grand Master Test: 12-question mixed assessment across all 15 levels.
  4. Graduation Trophy & Phonics Master Certificate!
